7 Under-Sink Storage & Organisation Ideas

Turn an awkward cupboard into a practical storage zone with simple ideas that work around pipes and limited space.

Quick summary: Measure first, work around plumbing, group similar products together and choose organisers that make items at the back easy to reach.

The cupboard under a kitchen or bathroom sink can quickly become one of the messiest areas in the home. Pipes, cleaning bottles and awkward corners make it difficult to use efficiently. These seven ideas focus on making the space easier to access rather than simply filling it with more containers.

1. Measure around the plumbing first

Before buying any organiser, measure the cupboard width, depth and height as well as the position of pipes. This helps you avoid storage products that look useful online but do not actually fit your space.

2. Use narrow pull-out organisers

A slim pull-out organiser can make products at the back easier to reach. Two narrower units can sometimes work better than one wide unit because they can sit on either side of the plumbing.

3. Group products by purpose

Create simple categories such as dishwashing, general cleaning, rubbish bags and spare supplies. Keeping related products together makes it easier to find what you need and notice when something is running low.

4. Use the inside of the cupboard door

If your door and hinges allow it, lightweight hooks or a slim door organiser can hold cloths, gloves or other small items. Make sure anything attached to the door does not hit the plumbing or shelves when closed.

5. Put frequently used products at the front

Daily-use items should be the easiest to access. Backup products and less frequently used supplies can sit further back or on a higher level.

6. Add a small shelf or stackable system

If there is unused vertical space, a small shelf can create a second level. Choose a design that can be positioned around pipes and still allows you to remove items without emptying the cupboard.

7. Keep the floor of the cupboard easy to clean

A removable tray or washable liner can help protect the cabinet from small leaks or spills. Avoid packing the cupboard so tightly that you cannot easily check the plumbing or clean the base.

Small-space tip: You do not need matching containers. A few correctly sized organisers that fit around your plumbing are usually more useful than a complete decorative storage set.

Before you buy an organiser

Take a photo of your cupboard and write down its measurements. When we add product recommendations later, this will also be an important point for readers: check dimensions before purchasing.
